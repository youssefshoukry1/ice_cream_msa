## Selling rules for approval

- **Size and recipe.** Use the specific normal and Mega prices above. Most adult cups have 3 or 6 scoops. ERDBEER JOGHURT B. and FRÜCHTE JOGHURT B. have 2 or 4. Offer a flavor choice on KINDER PINOCCHIO, EIS MAUS, MILCHSHAKE and the €2.20 Eiskugel portion. PANDA BÄR and MARIENKÄFER retain their named flavors. Both Spaghetti Eis recipes require pressed vanilla.
- **Cream substitution.** Offer “ohne Sahne” with one additional scoop at the same price on page 2 adult desserts. Children’s cups are excluded from the bonus. Among drinks the flyer expressly grants it on EISSCHOKOLADE and EISCAFÉ. Confirm how the kitchen chooses the bonus flavor.
- **Availability.** Staff control current stock for rotating flavors, seasonal desserts and BERRY TANGO. Unavailable flavors should be disabled everywhere a customer picks a scoop. Confirm prepared-dessert availability separately with the kitchen.
- **Extras.** Confirm which dishes accept each portion, which portions can be bought alone, and which grouped lines represent separate choices. Keep optional paid extras distinct from the ingredients included in a recipe.
- **Packaging.** Where offered, show the reusable drink cup deposit of €2 or ice cream container deposit of €5 as a separate refundable charge. Confirm eligible containers and quantities per order.
- **Delivery.** The flyer gives Norderstedt only, a €14 minimum, and €2 delivery. Proposed threshold uses merchandise and selected extras before delivery and deposits. Confirm this calculation, address coverage and off-season hours. Printed prices include VAT. Item-level tax rates require business confirmation.
- **Brand and ingredient information.** Proposed storefront identity is Eis-Taxi, crediting Eiscafé Pinocchio as printed. Confirm the public name before replacing Löffelwerk. Obtain the missing footnote 12 definition and confirm the differing Amarena cherry references before publishing affected ingredient information.

## Sample customer journey

1. In **Spaghetti Eis**, choose **SPAGHETTI EIS**, normal, for **8,50 €**. Select “ohne Sahne”. Add the extra scoop to the preparation note at no charge.
2. In **Kinderbecher**, add **MARIENKÄFER** for **6,50 €**. The cart shows the fixed size and the two printed flavors.
3. Merchandise totals **15,00 €**, above the €14 minimum. For an eligible Norderstedt address with standard packaging, the €2 delivery fee brings the total to **17,00 €**. Carry both items and the preparation note through checkout to the kitchen order.
