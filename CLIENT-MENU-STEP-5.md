## Review a price change

1. Open **Browse products by collection → Spaghetti Eis → SPAGHETTI EIS**.
2. Expand **Portions and prices → normal**. The value **850** represents €8.50. **Mega** has its own value of **1530**.
3. For an approved price revision, edit only the intended portion and wait for Studio to save the draft. The same draft appears in Eisbecher. During this review, leave the record in draft status.

All 67 menu records use the approved library and reference the source flyer position. The 38 printed extras rows sit within the eight portion groups. Staff can assign eligible extra groups after the client confirms combinations. Order availability starts switched off for the review. The storefront connection is Step 6 and release approval is Step 8.
