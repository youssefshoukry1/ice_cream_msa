# Preliminary visual identity proposal

**Modern Playful Premium** gives flavor photography room to be expressive and makes the practical details of buying ice cream predictable. Righteous Gelato places illustration around the product, Eiswerk Brehna aligns size and price, and Salt & Straw uses close-ups to show texture. The final treatment depends on the client’s packaging and photography. [Partner case study](https://www.heypartner.co/work/righteous-gelato) · [Eiswerk Brehna](https://eiswerk-brehna.de/) · [Salt & Straw](https://saltandstraw.com/collections/all-flavors)

## Color roles

| Role | Color | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Primary surface | Warm Cream `#FFF8EF` | Reading surfaces and a common background across flavors |
| Primary ink and action | Deep Chocolate `#35251F` | Headings, prices, body copy and primary buttons with cream text |
| Signature accent | Strawberry Pink `#E96B86` | Flavor panels or seasonal details with dark text |
| Secondary | Pistachio Green `#DCE9B5` | A backdrop for a darker green pack or an ingredient detail |
| Secondary | Vanilla Yellow `#FFE5A3` | Warm seasonal imagery. Check the edge separation of pale products. |
| Secondary | Sky Blue `#D9EBF5` | A cool backdrop for warm-colored scoops or delivery information |

All six colors from the initial hypothesis have a role. Contrast calculations give cream on chocolate **13.88:1**, chocolate on pink **4.81:1**, and white on pink **3.04:1**. Use the dark button for the main purchase action and chocolate lettering on pink. Give each flavor one accent and a visible name. Test pale packs against their backdrops under the actual studio lighting.

## Typography and German copy

- **Fraunces**, weight 550–650, for campaign and collection headlines. Its curved forms suggest craft. Start at 48–64 px on desktop and 32–40 px on mobile. [Fraunces](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fraunces)
- **DM Sans**, weight 400–500 for body copy and 600–700 for prices and buttons. Start body text at 16 px with roughly 1.5 line height. Manrope overlaps with the role DM Sans already fills. [DM Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans)
- Test **Ä Ö Ü ä ö ü ß €** in context. Use *Weiße Schokolade*, *Mango-Passionsfruchteis* and *Lieferinformationen* to check wrapping and line height. Let names wrap, keep values with their units (e.g. *13,80 €/l*), and give required information enough size and contrast to read on mobile.

## Interface and spacing

Use a 1200–1320 px content width, with 48–64 px margins on wide screens and 16–20 px on phones. Allow around 8 px between closely related values such as size and price, and 16–24 px between groups. Start with four products across on wide screens. Test two across on mobile with the longest flavor name and enlarged text. Switch to a wider card if the price wraps awkwardly or the button becomes cramped.

Use a square pack image, a fine border and 12–16 px card corners. Reserve shadows for floating layers. Give *In den Warenkorb* a dark button about 48 px high. Use both an outline and check mark for the selected size, and spell out *Ausverkauft*. Label icons where their meaning is ambiguous. Ratings and certifications require evidence.

## Photography brief

1. Start with one light and one dark tub. Match lens, camera distance and three-quarter angle. Use diffuse side lighting and a subtle contact shadow. Check that the flavor label and pack format remain recognizable at catalog size.
2. Add one macro image per flavor showing a real characteristic, such as a fruit swirl or pistachio pieces. Lead with the pack for comparison and follow with texture for closer inspection.
3. Use a hand or spoon in editorial images to establish scale. Frame wide enough for a mobile crop that preserves the product name. Have the client verify ingredient styling and color against the actual ice cream.

## German shopping requirements

**Price and quantity.** An illustrative pack might read *500 ml · 6,90 € · 13,80 €/l*. Update both prices when size changes, and position tax and delivery information near the price as applicable. For goods covered by the rules, the PAngV requires a clearly legible Grundpreis. The standard unit for prepacked goods sold by volume is one liter. Check exceptions and the composition of each bundle. [PAngV § 4](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/pangv_2022/__4.html) · [PAngV § 5](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/pangv_2022/__5.html)

**Ingredients and allergens.** Give shoppers access to *Zutaten*, *Allergene*, *Nährwerte* and other applicable mandatory information before purchase. Emphasize allergens within the ingredients list, for example through font weight. A *vegan* label alone does not answer an allergy question. For prepacked food sold at a distance, the minimum durability or use-by date is excepted from the pre-purchase information requirement. [European Commission guidance on distance selling](https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/labelling-and-nutrition/food-information-consumers-legislation/distance-selling_en) · [Food information legislation](https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/labelling-and-nutrition/food-information-consumers-legislation_en)

**Kühlversand.** Show postcode eligibility, dispatch days, minimum order and shipping cost before the shopper starts assembling a box. If dispatch pauses for winter, make the action reflect what can be ordered. Use *Versandinformationen* to explain packaging, receipt and what to do if a delivery arrives thawed. The client must supply the actual policy. [NOMOO](https://nomoo.de/collections/shop) · [Eiswerk Brehna](https://eiswerk-brehna.de/)

**Future Stripe checkout.** Carry the German language, item descriptions, sizes, quantities, EUR totals and shipping charges through from the cart. Keep a clear route back to edit the order. Confirm the payment methods supported by the client’s account before advertising them.

## Decisions requiring client approval

- Endorse a preliminary direction and identify whether everyday purchases, premium gifts or both drive the business.
- Review Fraunces and DM Sans, the dark purchase button and one proposed accent beside the actual packaging before fixing the rest of the palette.
- Confirm photography treatment, budget, sample packs, logo assets and existing image rights. Decide how much brand identity should come from the wrapper and how much from presentation.
- Supply flavors, pack sizes, prices, bundle rules and verified allergen information. Confirm the minimum economical order, service areas, dispatch days and policy for thawed deliveries.
- Choose *du* or *Sie* for German customer copy and substantiate origin or quality claims. Phase 2 requires explicit approval.
