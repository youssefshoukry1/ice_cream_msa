# Visual identity research for a German ice cream store

**Preliminary recommendation: Modern Playful Premium.** Eiswerk Brehna shows how size, price per liter and the purchase action can fit in a compact card. Hackney Gelato shows how a consistent photo setup can still make flavors easy to distinguish. Together they suggest a division of work for this store. Give the image a flavor-specific identity and keep purchase details in familiar positions. [Brehna product grid](screenshots/brehna-grid.png) · [Hackney flavor lineup](screenshots/hackney-flavors.png)

## Method and scope

The review started with the suggested stores and agency case studies, then expanded to German makers, gelato catalogs, chocolate and beverage brands. The screening considered how clearly each source presented a product and how much of the buying decision the shopper could see. Nine references were selected for distinct contributions to color, photography, typography and purchase information. The [source register](references.md) documents the full shortlist, additional sources and capture limitations.

Desktop screenshots share one viewport. Mobile comparisons address two specific questions: when the product first appears at Van Leeuwen and whether adjacent cards remain readable at Eiswerk Brehna. Font names come from computed page styles, except for the description of the design pictured inside Partner’s case study, which is visual. Palette proposals are independent design choices. The client’s packaging, conversion data and user-test results were unavailable. The judgments below concern visual suitability. Phase 1 covers identity research.

## Individual reference analysis

### Eiswerk Brehna

The narrow Instrument Serif headline gives “Handgemachtes Eis. Direkt nach Hause.” the character of a shop sign. Archivo handles sizes and prices. A pale tub sits on a white card against ivory, while a rectangular black button makes the next action easy to find. Four desktop cards align the size selector, price, Grundpreis and button. The mobile grid keeps two columns, leaving less room for long German names. The seasonal Winterpause notice also shows why shipping availability belongs near the start of the visit. [Original capture](screenshots/brehna.png) · [Eiswerk Brehna](https://eiswerk-brehna.de/)

### Van Leeuwen

A dark rule divides the sandy text panel from the cream product panel. A turquoise arch separates the yellow scoops from the background. A pink oval SHOP NOW button gains definition from its dark outline and offset shadow. SofiaPro gives the flavor name more weight than the navigation. On mobile the copy and button precede the photograph. Keep German campaign copy short enough for the tub to appear early, and label local pickup separately if the client offers it. [Original capture](screenshots/van-leeuwen.png) · [Van Leeuwen](https://vanleeuwenicecream.com/)

### Salt & Straw

Berry swirls and chocolate flecks fill rounded square photographs in a four-column grid. White space, thin rules and dark Inter text give each flavor the same visual weight. Sold Out occupies the same position as the add button and price on available products. A shopper can compare texture and availability at a glance. Pair this kind of close-up with a package image so the buyer can also judge the format, and reserve space for multi-line flavor names. [Original capture](screenshots/salt-flavors.png) · [Salt & Straw](https://saltandstraw.com/collections/all-flavors)

### NOMOO

The yellow Stieleis section sits beneath orange navigation and a turquoise category bar. The basket repeats the turquoise. PP Agrandir gives short headings a playful voice. Pack shots, ratings, prices and small plus buttons repeat across the row. White detail text is harder to read on yellow, and the basket takes up considerable horizontal space. Borrow the early Mindestbestellwert message, then give purchase details a calmer surface and a clearly labeled action. [Original capture](screenshots/nomoo.png) · [NOMOO](https://nomoo.de/collections/shop)

### Tano Gelato

A hand holding a cup against blue sky gives the buyer a sense of its size. The arched photograph and pale Syne headline share a broad orange field. Two light pill-shaped buttons give Deliveroo and Uber Eats equal prominence. The composition suits an origin story. A direct-sales store would benefit from one primary purchase action. Test the expressive headline with German compounds before using it across a catalog. [Original capture](screenshots/tano.png) · [Tano Gelato](https://www.tanogelato.com/)

### Hackney Gelato

Three tubs stand against white, with two scoops above each rim. Green, purple and orange come from the packaging. Condensed uppercase names and short descriptions sit underneath, separated by space instead of heavy borders. Page styles specify Barlow Condensed. The award emblem touches the pack shot and depends on a substantiated award. The catalog view supplies neither price nor purchase action. Borrow the consistent camera angle, then allow wider lettering for long German names. [Original capture](screenshots/hackney-flavors.png) · [Hackney Gelato](https://www.hackneygelato.com/gelato)

### Jeni’s

A cream panel groups ALL FLAVORS with shipping assurances. Selection starts below on white. Moderat changes size and weight between headings and detail text. Small icons mark delivery benefits, outlined ADD buttons repeat beneath pack shots, and a separate area tracks the collection. This pattern earns its space if frozen delivery requires a minimum number of tubs. Keep that condition legible alongside the picker and simplify German navigation labels. [Original capture](screenshots/jenis-flavors.png) · [Jeni’s](https://jenis.com/pages/all-flavors?source=pageEmbed&page=addProductsPage1&currentFlow=byob#hang_open)

### Righteous Gelato × Partner

Inside the agency’s dark frame, a cream composition centers a deep-green serif headline and three tubs. Flat yellow, coral and green botanical forms occupy the edges. A small coral button appears in the top bar. The open center keeps the lettering and packaging readable. Apply that rule to flavor campaigns and allow long German names their own text area. This image documents the agency’s design. It provides no evidence about the current cart interaction. [Original capture](screenshots/righteous-brand.png) · [Righteous Gelato × Partner](https://www.heypartner.co/work/righteous-gelato)

### Original Beans

An overhead chocolate-pack arrangement sits beside a navy Christmas Specials panel. Dark green and cream frame navigation. A gold action stands out against navy, while Instrument Sans distinguishes a large headline from a much smaller description. The image gives a gift set presence, although price and complete contents are absent from the slide. Reserve this treatment for a defined gift range and allow room for a longer German description. [Original capture](screenshots/original-beans.png) · [Original Beans](https://originalbeans.com/)

## What changes on mobile

At Van Leeuwen, a panel that occupied half the desktop screen moves below the headline and button. A longer German flavor name or seasonal description could push the tub below the fold. Test a two-line name with enough packaging still visible to recognize the product. At Brehna, two columns preserve side-by-side comparison. The limiting factor is the space needed for size selectors and Grundpreis. Give the card more width if the price breaks or the action becomes cramped. [Van Leeuwen mobile](screenshots/van-mobile.png) · [Brehna mobile](screenshots/brehna-mobile-grid.png)

## Three visual directions

| Direction | Flavor recognition | Purchase information | Main dependency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minimal Artisan | Texture and differences between packs | Neutral fields give prices priority | Distinctive photography and a credible maker story |
| Bold & Playful | Strong colors and large shapes | Details need a quieter surface | Short names and an audience receptive to a lively voice |
| Modern Playful Premium | Flavor accents and consistent photography | Cards reserve space for comparison | A repeatable shoot and a recognizable pack |

### Minimal Artisan

A neutral setting lets packaging, light and texture convey how the ice cream was made. Instrument Serif for short headlines and Archivo for product details. Use straight-edged cards, fine borders, a consistent background and soft side lighting.

Proposed palette — `#F6F1E9` · `#27231F` · `#9C846C` · `#BFC7A7`

Strong for comparing prices and sizes or telling a local maker’s story. Similar pale tubs can be difficult to distinguish at a distance. This direction depends on distinctive photographs and a documented origin story. [Review the screenshot board](moodboards/index.html#A)

### Bold & Playful

A saturated field and a single memorable image suit seasonal campaigns with short names. Syne for display and DM Sans for purchasing. Pair broad color fields and rounded buttons with cutout packs or a hand-held cup.

Proposed palette — `#F4BF16` · `#E95C36` · `#49A8B6` · `#31221F`

Quick flavor recognition suits a youthful range or ice pops. Saturated surfaces and a persistent basket compete for space on mobile. German prices and allergen details need a quiet reading area. [Review the screenshot board](moodboards/index.html#B)

### Modern Playful Premium

Give each flavor its own image treatment while keeping the name, size, price and action in predictable positions. Fraunces for display and DM Sans for body text and prices. Use restrained curves, consistent pack angles and a second photograph to show texture.

Proposed palette — `#FFF8EF` · `#35251F` · `#E96B86` · `#DCE9B5` · `#FFE5A3` · `#D9EBF5`

Balances product personality with room for German copy and shipping details. It requires consistent photography and disciplined color use. The packaging must supply enough character to distinguish the brand. [Review the screenshot board](moodboards/index.html#C)

## Recommendation and conditions for reconsideration

Modern Playful Premium best addresses the two demands visible in the references: showing how the flavors differ and making German purchase and delivery information easy to read. If the client uses neutral tubs, a flavor-specific photo background can carry the accent. Minimal Artisan deserves another look if verified local provenance anchors a premium range. Bold & Playful becomes more persuasive if single-serve seasonal items and illustrated wrappers are the brand’s strongest assets.
