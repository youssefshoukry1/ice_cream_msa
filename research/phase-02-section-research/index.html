Header and navigation

Help visitors find the shop and check whether frozen dispatch is available.

Why this reference

The narrow black dispatch notice stays visually distinct from the white navigation row. The shop link sits near the wordmark, while account and cart controls occupy the opposite end.

Adaptation for the German store

Use a short, operational message when dispatch dates change. Give Eissorten and Versand clear labels, keep the cart visible on small screens, and include business services only if the client sells them.

Hero

Introduce one flavor and provide a direct route to shopping.

Why this reference

The scoop and labeled tub identify the product at a glance. The separate text panel keeps the headline and oval button readable. On mobile, copy and action stack above the pack.

Adaptation for the German store

Test a real German flavor name with the client’s pack shot. Keep the description short enough for the tub to enter the first mobile screen. Use Sorten entdecken for a range or a flavor-specific action for a single available product.

Alternative for an evergreen hero — Brehna pairs the maker’s proposition with a shoppable tub when there is no seasonal launch.

Product categories

Help buyers choose a format before comparing individual flavors.

Why this reference

Four pack silhouettes distinguish a small tub, larger tub, bundle and ice pops. A shopper can recognize the format before reading the label.

Adaptation for the German store

Name groups by the distinction a buyer cares about. If the same flavor simply comes in two sizes, keep those sizes within one product and use broader category labels such as Eisbecher and Probierboxen. Pair every picture with a readable German label.

Alternative for a small range — Brehna groups flavors with compact filters and keeps size selection on each card.

Featured products

Offer a manageable starting selection for undecided buyers.

Why this reference

The bestseller row repeats a tub photograph, rating, name, price and add control. Consistent scale makes the selection feel like one family. The partial next card suggests more flavors beyond the viewport.

Adaptation for the German store

Feature a small assortment that spans genuinely different tastes. Use Unsere Auswahl until sales data supports Bestseller. Keep the action distinguishable from ratings and give the complete assortment a visible link.

Product cards

Let buyers compare quantity and cost before adding a tub.

Why this reference

The four-card row aligns pack photographs, size options, total price, unit price and a dark add button. The mobile capture shows two cards across with those elements still grouped.

Adaptation for the German store

Update price and Grundpreis together when a size changes. Test Mango-Passionsfruchteis and In den Warenkorb together at phone width and larger text sizes. Use a wider card whenever the controls become cramped.

Brand story

Explain who makes the ice cream and what distinguishes it.

Why this reference

The maker’s story gets its own colored panel. A short origin label leads into a short narrative about Argentine gelato, the founder and small-batch production in Hackney.

Adaptation for the German store

Ask the client for one verifiable detail about the maker, place or process. Pair a short paragraph with a real production image and an Über uns link. Keep the shopping action elsewhere so the story has space to read.

Promotional section

Give a seasonal collection a recognizable occasion and destination.

Why this reference

The Christmas Specials slide pairs a photographed gift arrangement with a navy text field. Its gold button points toward a collection while keeping lettering clear of the busy packaging.

Adaptation for the German store

For a frozen gift box, show what is inside, name the occasion and give the last dispatch date once confirmed. Use one action such as Geschenkbox ansehen. A static seasonal panel is sufficient when only one offer is active.

Customer reviews

Help buyers picture the flavor through someone else’s experience.

Why this reference

A single attributed testimonial, portrait and short quote occupy a turquoise speech bubble. The quote is readable without opening a product. This is a brand-curated testimonial display.

Adaptation for the German store

Use permissioned feedback and identify the relevant flavor where possible. Show a source or verification label only when supported. Make the quote readable without rotating the panel. With no feedback at launch, leave this section out.

FAQ

Resolve ordering questions before the buyer abandons a basket.

Why this reference

Three visible answers address refrigerated delivery, free shipping and the location of ingredient and allergen details. A separate link leads to the fuller delivery policy.

Adaptation for the German store

Use Fragen zur Bestellung and answer with the client’s actual delivery area, minimum order and thawed-delivery policy. Link directly to product-specific allergen details. A short list can stay expanded on mobile.

Footer

Provide dependable routes to service, business details and policies.

Why this reference

The dark footer groups Shop, Service and Rechtliches links under distinct headings. A visitor looking for delivery terms or contact details can scan one column.

Adaptation for the German store

Give Versand & Lieferung and Kontakt prominent service links. Keep German policy names recognizable, including Impressum and Datenschutz. The reference has several B2B groups; reserve that much space only when the client has comparable services.
