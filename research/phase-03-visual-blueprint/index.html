One visual system

Warm Cream #FFF8EFDeep Chocolate #35251FStrawberry #E96B86Pistachio #DCE9B5Vanilla #FFE5A3Sky #D9EBF5

Type and colorSet campaign titles in Fraunces and purchasing text in DM Sans. Use cream for reading, chocolate for prices and primary actions, and a single accent per flavor. Reserve blue for delivery cues.

Photography and shapeUse a consistent three-quarter pack angle and diffuse side light. Add a scoop or macro detail to communicate texture. Keep square pack frames and 12–16 px card corners consistent across the range.

Spacing and readabilityUse a 1200–1320 px content width and 48–64 px desktop gutters. On phones use 16–20 px gutters, 16 px body text and approximately 48 px purchase controls. Keep price and unit together.

Annotated reference decisions

The numbered outline identifies the part of each source screenshot that informed the proposal. Open any image for the unmarked original.

Arrival

Navigation & dispatch

Keep Eissorten, Versand and Über uns in the main navigation. Give Warenkorb its own position. Show a dispatch notice only when there is a meaningful update.

On mobile Collapse secondary links under Menü. Keep Warenkorb visible and let a dispatch message wrap.

Client input Actual trading name, logo and delivery status.

Discovery

Flavor-led hero

Pair one short German headline with one pack-and-scoop photograph. Sorten entdecken leads to the selection on this page. Use vanilla yellow or one other accent chosen for contrast with the pack.

On mobile Lead with the flavor name, keep the photograph immediately below it, then place the action. This moves the image earlier than in the reference's mobile stack.

Client input One available lead flavor, licensed pack photography and approved headline.

Browse

Format choices

Use a compact row of labeled format choices ahead of the assortment. Selecting a format narrows the products below. If the catalog launches with tubs alone, omit this row and keep size inside the product card.

On mobile Wrap choices into two columns. Keep every label visible without horizontal scrolling.

Client input Actual product formats and enough products to justify a filter.

Shop

Featured assortment

Start with Unsere Auswahl and up to four distinct flavors on cream. Use one consistent pack scale. Alle Sorten leads to the full range. Reserve Bestseller for a ranking the business can support.

On mobile Use two columns when labels fit. Show the full curated selection without requiring a swipe.

Client input Sellable assortment, stock, and a merchandising choice for the opening selection.

Shop

Product card detail

Repeat one anatomy inside the assortment. Let the name open details including Zutaten and Allergene. A size change updates total price and Grundpreis together. In den Warenkorb adds the selected variant. Replace the action with Ausverkauft when unavailable.

On mobile Keep the unit price beside the total or directly below. Switch to one column when enlarged type or a compound name squeezes the control.

Client input Net quantity, price, unit price, tax and shipping wording, ingredient data and stock per variant.

Seasonal

Gift or seasonal offer

Follow the main selection with one quieter seasonal panel. Describe the box contents and link with Geschenkbox ansehen. Omit the panel if there is no active offer, leaving the maker story next.

On mobile Stack a compact image above the offer and keep the confirmed dispatch deadline near the action.

Client input Available gift SKU, real contents, assets and confirmed dispatch cutoff.

Origin

Maker story

Use cream with a pistachio accent. Pair one real production photograph with a specific fact about the maker or process. Link to Über uns. Place the story after shopping so returning customers can reach the tubs first.

On mobile Keep the origin statement and photo together. Move longer history to the linked page.

Client input Verified maker story, location and rights to a production photograph.

Trust

Customer voice

Use one permissioned flavor-specific quote beneath the maker story, with its actual source. Omit this position until real feedback is available. The purchase journey still works without it.

On mobile Display a static quote with visible attribution. Any portrait is optional.

Client input Permission, original wording, source and accurate verification status.

Delivery

Ordering FAQ

Use Fragen zur Bestellung for Kühlversand, minimum order and receiving a thawed parcel. Link to the delivery policy and direct shoppers to the relevant product for allergens. Link here from the early shipping cue.

On mobile Keep the first delivery answer visible. Keep headings short enough to scan and the policy link within reach.

Client input Service area, charges, order minimum, dispatch days and handling policy.

Service

Footer

Close with a chocolate surface and cream text. Group Sortiment, Service and Rechtliches, with Kontakt, Versand & Lieferung, Impressum and Datenschutz easy to locate.

On mobile Stack groups in reading order with Service prominent and adequate spacing between links.

Client input Company details, contact channels, approved policies and final URLs.

Decisions for review

  • Merchandising. Confirm a flavor-led opening and a curated assortment as the primary shopping path. If gifting is the main business, move the gift offer directly below the hero.
  • Brand expression. Review the palette beside real packaging, approve the photography treatment, and choose du or Sie before final German copy.
  • Launch content. Confirm which formats, gifts, maker claims and testimonials have supporting assets. Remove conditional sections where material is unavailable.
  • Ordering facts. Supply variant prices and quantities, ingredient and allergen records, service area, minimum order, charges and dispatch terms. These determine the finished product cards and shipping messages.
